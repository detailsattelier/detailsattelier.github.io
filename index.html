About De'tails Attelier

Hello! I'm Stefen — an aspiring fashion creative with a big dream of making a name in the world of fashion. This is where my journey begins.

I develop fashion concepts through digital illustration, exploring silhouettes, fabric textures, embellishments, and thoughtful color stories. My creative process is built around the small details that give each design its own character.

Created with ibisPaint on a tablet, each artwork reflects my ongoing exploration as an independent fashion illustrator.

“Every detail tells a story.”
